
## Sequence 9: Marauder\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FError_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**

**Marauders** are difficult to separate from ordinary bandits or thieves. Perhaps the means available to them are more impressive, but their goal of stealing riches is not for enjoyment or survival - for them, it is more like answering a calling or passion.<sup id="cite_ref-1.65_1-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.65-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

**Traits**

-   **Physical Enhancement**: After advancing, they will gain a certain degree of physical improvement.

-   **Combat Proficiency**: They are good at using short weapons, such as daggers and short swords.

-   **Agile Hands**: The stability and flexibility of their wrist and fingers have enhanced.
    -   This makes their hands known for their speed and agility.

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_2_2-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_2-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.65_1-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.65-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.287_4-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.287-4" aria-label="Reference 4"><span>[</span>4<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.286_5-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.286-5" aria-label="Reference 5"><span>[</span>5<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Superior Observation**: They are be able to directly sense valuable items within a _ten meter range_, but can't determine where they exactly are.<sup id="cite_ref-6"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-6" aria-label="Reference 6"><span>[</span>6<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   This intuition can be so powerful, that they can sense if there is something valuable in a drawer or cupboard, without actually being capable of seeing what's inside.
        -   They can sense if there is a concealed target by sensing the valuables they have.<sup id="cite_ref-2.861_7-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.861-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Theft** (**Stealing**): They can easily **Steal** items from a target's pockets without being detected.

## Sequence 8: Swindler\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FError_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Description**

These **Beyonders** like to take part in committing fraud. They gain enjoyment from swindling.

**Traits**

-   **Charm**: They possess strong **Charm** that makes them more approachable and **Charming**.
    -   The **Swindler's Charm** makes people believe them easily.

-   **Physical Enhancement**: They have become much more agile and fast.
    -   They are quick in action and fast in speed when executing matters related to their plans.
    -   They can somersault out of areas and dodge the **Paper Daggers** of a **[Clown](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Clown "Fool Pathway/Abilities")**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.287_4-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.287-4" aria-label="Reference 4"><span>[</span>4<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.286_5-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.286-5" aria-label="Reference 5"><span>[</span>5<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.65_1-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.65-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_2_2-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_2-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.65_1-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.65-1" aria-label="Reference 1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Eloquence**: Their **Eloquence** includes both the simple improvement of speech techniques and the addition of **Beyonder** effects that make people convinced.
    -   "They" use the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to influence the **Body of Heart and Mind**.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_fool_authorities_8-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_fool_authorities-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Thought Misdirection**: This can be achieved through their **Eloquence**, as well as be accomplished through their actions, postures, surrounding environment, arrangements, etc.
    -   Likewise, a skilled **Swindler** can **Misled** others even without using the **Beyonders** abilities related to **Thought Misdirection**.

-   **Mental Disruption**: A **Swindler** can cause certain hallucinations to be experienced by a target.

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_2_2-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_2-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Superior Observation**: In addition to being used to sense valuables, a **Swindler** can use their **Superior Observation** to see a target person's micro-expressions and small movements.
    -   Although it is not as powerful as a **[Spectator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Spectator)**, it is enough to grasp the target's mental state.

-   **Theft (Stealing)**: They can **Steal** some spiritual materials from a shorter distance.<sup id="cite_ref-9"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-9" aria-label="Reference 9"><span>[</span>9<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   However, the premise is that spiritual material interacts with the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
        -   [Hazel Macht](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hazel_Macht "Hazel Macht") had **Stolen** a spiritual material that was buried under the dirt through the dirt without having to excavate the buried area and getting it herself physically.

**Pathway Versions Difference**

-   [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Pathway** **Version** of **Sequence 8 Swindler** lacks the qualitative transformation of **Superior Observation** and only gains it upon becoming a **Sequence 4** **Parasite**.
    -   When encountering [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon"), except for gifted **Swindlers**, such as [Lanevus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lanevus "Lanevus"), even if "He" is merely pranking without using "His" **Beyonder** powers, they cannot see through it which makes them a target of mockery.

## Sequence 7: Cryptologist\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FError_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**New Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_setting_2_2-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_setting_2-2" aria-label="Reference 2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Decryption**: **Cryptologists** can recreate the truth behind anything related to mysticism or predict the development of events just from bits and scraps of evidence.
    -   There are three aspects that make up this ability:
        1.  Their ability to observe, associate thoughts, and deduce is improved.
        2.  Their own **Spiritual Intuition** has been massively enhanced.
        3.  They gain the mastery of mysticism knowledge.
    -   **Decryption** is not **Divination** or **Prophecy**. A **Cryptologist** needs enough information for deduction and they can’t make wild guesses.<sup id="cite_ref-10"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-10" aria-label="Reference 10"><span>[</span>10<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They need to be wary as some **Decryption** efforts may bring attention from high-level beings.
    -   They can **Decrypt** dreams, **Illusions**, various mysteries and the location of enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-1.981_11-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.981-11" aria-label="Reference 11"><span>[</span>11<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They can **Decrypt** the incantations to activate various charms and [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item").<sup id="cite_ref-12"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-12" aria-label="Reference 12"><span>[</span>12<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   They can **Decrypt** the secrets behind [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") and [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact").<sup id="cite_ref-13"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-13" aria-label="Reference 13"><span>[</span>13<span>]</span></a></sup>

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Superior Observation**: It now also includes an additional perception of subtle clues and faint traces.

**Pathway Versions Difference**

-   [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Pathway Version** does not have this additional enhancement until becoming a **Parasite**, which results in many **Cryptologists** having incomplete and insufficiently sharp abilities.

## Sequence 6: Prometheus\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FError_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**Traits**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Physical Enhancement**: They gain additional physique, strength, and speed enhancements.

-   **Mental Resistance**: They have an increased resistance towards mental **Corruption**.

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-14"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-14" aria-label="Reference 14"><span>[</span>14<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.612_15-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.612-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Theft** (**Stealing**): From **Sequence 6** onward, not only would **Theft** have its effects and success rate increase, but it would also obtain a deeper level of conceptualization. A **Prometheus's** **Theft** can affect **Beyonder** powers and objects across a distance.
    -   **Prometheus** has two types of **Theft**:
        1.  Within _50 meters radius_, A **Prometheus** can temporarily **Steal** a target's **Beyonder** power and use it themselves with proficiency for about _10 minutes_. Once they have selected the ability they wish to **Steal**, by simply flicking their wrists, they detach the ability and cause it to fly towards them swiftly.
            -   There are several factors involved when it comes to **Stealing** powers.
                -   The more one understands the target, the easier it is to **Steal** the specific desired one; the more ignorant one is, the closer it is to random, depending on **Luck**.
                    -   Depending on **Luck** means sensing various colored lights on the target's body and then making a choice.
                    -   They must be able to locate the corresponding symbols that make up the desired ability, which takes time if they don’t know what to look for.
                        -   While locating the corresponding symbols/colored lights, they are able to differentiate abilities that are being actively used from those that are not through sensing the changes in the colored lights.<sup id="cite_ref-2.585_16-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.585-16" aria-label="Reference 16"><span>[</span>16<span>]</span></a></sup>
                -   When the target's **Sequence** is higher than 6, failed results may occur; the further up, the greater the probability of failure.
                    -   **High-Sequence** **Beyonders** can also be **Stolen** from; however, the probability of success is smaller.
                -   Knowledge of the victim's **Pathway** and the corresponding mysticism is vitally important to increase their chances of a desired **Theft**.
            -   A successful **Theft** will make their target lose the **Stolen** power and would need anywhere from a _couple of hours_ to a _couple of days_ to recover.
                -   The minimum time to recover will be at least _twelve hours_.<sup id="cite_ref-1.612_15-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.612-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   A **Stolen** ability will continue to function if activated prior to the **Theft**, preventing it from being a way to stop foreign **Beyonder** powers.<sup id="cite_ref-2.585_16-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.585-16" aria-label="Reference 16"><span>[</span>16<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   Mental **Corruption** can be considered a kind of **Beyonder** power and thus can also be **Stolen**, albeit at one's own risk.
        2.  They can covertly **Steal** objects from a target within a _50 meter_ range, or conversely, covertly slip dangerous items into the pockets and hands of their target within the same range.<sup id="cite_ref-1.612_15-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.612-15" aria-label="Reference 15"><span>[</span>15<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   They can **Steal** more diverse things such as fire.<sup id="cite_ref-17"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-17" aria-label="Reference 17"><span>[</span>17<span>]</span></a></sup>
                -   At an unknown **Sequence**, they will become able to **Steal** natural phenomena such as **Lightning** and **Torrential Rain**; however, the premise is that they can withstand the **Stolen** phenomena.<sup id="cite_ref-18"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-18" aria-label="Reference 18"><span>[</span>18<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   Ownerless objects aren't hard to **Steal**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1380_19-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1380-19" aria-label="Reference 19"><span>[</span>19<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Superior Observation**: They can directly sense where valuable items are within a _50-meter range_, their approximate value, and possible types.
    -   Unless there are **Beyonder** powers of **Concealment** that can resist this type of perception, no treasure can escape a **Prometheus'** nose.

**Pathway Versions Difference**

-   [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") version lacks the second type of **Theft** and the transformation of **Superior Observation**, and its resistance to mental corruption is not enhanced until reaching **Sequence 4**.

## Sequence 5: Dream Stealer (Dreams Thief)\[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Flordofthemysteries.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FError_Pathway%2FAbilities%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")\]

**New Abilities**

-   **Disguise** (**[Theft of Heavenly Secrets](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Theft_of_Heavenly_Secrets "Western Continent/Glossary")**): They can **Disguise** themselves as a follower of a [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), bypass defenses, **Steal** prayer responses, and create all sorts of [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").
    -   In battle, through the use of longer incantations, pre-arrangements, or the **Stealing** of prayer responses, they can get some kind of enhancement or use some kind of abilities.
        -   [Harrison](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Harrison "Harrison") completed "**Theft of the Secrets of Heaven"**, accessing certain powers from the Samaritan Woman's Spring.
            -   For the last two sentences of the incantation, one sentence was <u>"<i><b><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Theft_of_Heavenly_Secrets" title="Western Continent/Glossary">Steal the Secrets of Heaven</a></b></i>"</u>, and the other was unclear at the beginning, ending with <u>"<i><b><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Theft_of_Heavenly_Secrets" title="Western Continent/Glossary">Swift as a decree, be driven</a></b></i>".</u><sup id="cite_ref-2.861_7-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-2.861-7" aria-label="Reference 7"><span>[</span>7<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   Through **Disguising** themselves as a follower of a [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), a **Dream Stealer** is able to create **Charms** of that corresponding [True Deity's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") domain.
    -   The higher the intelligence of the corresponding [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), the greater the probability for the **Disguise** to fail.

**Strengthened Abilities**<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_error_abilities_3-6"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_error_abilities-3" aria-label="Reference 3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Theft** (**Stealing**): A **Dream Stealer's** **Theft** has become more conceptualized, now allowing them to utilize **Theft** on thoughts, intentions, general abilities, knowledge, ideals, attacks, memories, and **Dreams** within _80 meters range_.
    -   Though they are unable to enter **Dreams**, **Dream Stealers** are able to infuse scenes within the **Dream** to influence their target's future decisions and actions.
        -   This is done by **Stealing,** storing, and transferring **Dreams**.<sup id="cite_ref-20"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-20" aria-label="Reference 20"><span>[</span>20<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-21"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-21" aria-label="Reference 21"><span>[</span>21<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-22"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-22" aria-label="Reference 22"><span>[</span>22<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   Through this they can also disrupt **Dream Divination**.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_death_abilities_q29_23-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_death_abilities_q29-23" aria-label="Reference 23"><span>[</span>23<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   **Thought Usurpation**: They can **Steal** the **Thoughts** the opponent is about to put into practice, causing them to experience a brief state of dullness.
        -   It is possible for the targets to completely miss the fact that **Thoughts** were **Stolen** by them, unless if they were to think about it carefully.
        -   At this stage, one cannot retain the **Stolen** thoughts and must instead perform the corresponding action in place of the **Stolen** target.
            -   [Mobet Zoroast](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mobet_Zoroast "Mobet Zoroast") **Stole** the [King of the North Ulyssan's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") intention to unleash a breath attack, causing the former to spit and the latter puzzled on what it wanted to do.
    -   They can **Steal** certain general abilities, such as the ability to walk, fly, speak, or even the ability to speak particular language.<sup id="cite_ref-24"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-24" aria-label="Reference 24"><span>[</span>24<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_coi_q9_25-0"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_coi_q9-25" aria-label="Reference 25"><span>[</span>25<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-1.981_11-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.981-11" aria-label="Reference 11"><span>[</span>11<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   However, those as well will take one of the **Theft** slots.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_coi_q9_25-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_coi_q9-25" aria-label="Reference 25"><span>[</span>25<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   [Anderson Hood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") used a [Mystical Item](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") related to the **Error Pathway** to **Steal** the ability to speak [Dutanese](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Languages "Languages") language from someone else.
    -   They can **Steal** the attacks targeting them in order to nullify them, and unless the attack itself has a side effect on the user, there won't be any side effect on the **Dream Stealer**.<sup id="cite_ref-26"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-26" aria-label="Reference 26"><span>[</span>26<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   If [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") hadn’t **Stolen** the corresponding damage dealt to [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), "He" would've been contaminated by the fog created after [Bladel's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bladel "Bladel") death.
            -   This would make "Him" become a **Cursed** entity that roams that area.<sup id="cite_ref-27"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-27" aria-label="Reference 27"><span>[</span>27<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   They can **Steal** a **[Reaper's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Reaper)** **Cull** or **Flame** without their body being burned by the **Flames**.
    -   They can **Steal** **Beyonder** powers within _80 meter range_.
        -   The corresponding **Stolen** **Beyonder** power can be used for _half_ _an_ _hour,_ but it cannot be used in different time periods.

    -   [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") completely **Deceived** [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") about "His" plans, leaving the latter having no way to realize the **Deception** until it was too late.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1385_30-6"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1385-30" aria-label="Reference 30"><span>[</span>30<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Deceived** [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle) in order to fulfill and create the corresponding requirement to complete the creation of a **Bug**.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1386_109-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1386-109" aria-label="Reference 109"><span>[</span>109<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   To resist the effects of **Blind Stupidity**, [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Deceived** the rules, lied to "Himself", and made "His" instincts believe that stopping [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") from leaving [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle) is what "He" wanted.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1384_49-4"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1384-49" aria-label="Reference 49"><span>[</span>49<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-124"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-124"><span>[</span>Error 1<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Theft** (**Stealing**): The authority of **Theft** is within the **Inconceivable** symbolism.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_chained_formulas_q16_60-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_chained_formulas_q16-60" aria-label="Reference 60"><span>[</span>60<span>]</span></a></sup> It is one of the core skills of the **Error Pathway**, and one of its main symbols. When an **Error** faces a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"), “They” can also use **Theft** that had reached the level of authority, **Stealing** the entire authority, including subordinate authorities.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1385_30-7"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1385-30" aria-label="Reference 30"><span>[</span>30<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-wechat_chained_formulas_q16_60-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_chained_formulas_q16-60" aria-label="Reference 60"><span>[</span>60<span>]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-125"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-125" aria-label="Reference 124"><span>[</span>124<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   It now has reached a deeper level of conceptualization; capable of of **Stealing** more specific aspects of a concept as well as have a higher success rate on most matters.
        -   A [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels") with the [![Error Symbol2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/07/Error_Symbol2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/26?cb=20260330061108)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway) can accurately **Steal** someone's "childhood" and "teenage" years, allowing them to instantly grow up. This is something that a **Worm of Time** couldn't accurately do.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1125_87-2"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1125-87" aria-label="Reference 87"><span>[</span>87<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   "They" can **Steal** "Their" **Spirit Body Threads** back from anyone trying to manipulate them.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1384_49-5"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1384-49" aria-label="Reference 49"><span>[</span>49<span>]</span></a></sup>
        -   The **Eternal Slumber** of **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness)** can be **Stolen** and **Gifted**.<sup id="cite_ref-126"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-126" aria-label="Reference 125"><span>[</span>125<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Decryption**: Stemming from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), "Their" power to **Decrypt** is massively improved, allowing "Them" to crack the secrets of many things from a tiny amount of information.<sup id="cite_ref-wechat_fool_authorities_8-1"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-wechat_fool_authorities-8" aria-label="Reference 8"><span>[</span>8<span>]</span></a></sup>
    -   "They" are able to instantly sense the secrets and general traits of environments "They" enter.
        -   [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") cracked the secrets and found **Loopholes** in the [**Fool’s**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Divine Kingdom** from the information "He" gained from just _2 attempts_ of breaking into it.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1382_112-3"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-1.1382-112" aria-label="Reference 112"><span>[</span>112<span>]</span></a></sup>
            -   In addition, "He" fully **Decrypted** [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") reasons for constructing a **Divine Kingdom**.

-   **Avatars**: **Avatars** belongs to the **Puppetry** authority,<sup id="cite_ref-127"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-127" aria-label="Reference 126"><span>[</span>126<span>]</span></a></sup> which is a subordinate authority of the **Bizarreness** and **Change** symbolism, plus the **Dominator of the Spirit World**.<sup id="cite_ref-128"><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#cite_note-128" aria-label="Reference 127"><span>[</span>127<span>]</span></a></sup>

-   **Time**: "They" have an authority over **Time**, derived from the **King of Space-Time**.
    -   **Space**: "They" have partial authority over **Space**.
        -   This is because **Space-Time** authorities cannot be completely separated.
